Getting started

Add your income, then what you owe, then your current bank balance. The Dashboard and Projection will show where you stand each pay period and how low your balance dips. Want to explore first? Choose “Try demo data” on the welcome screen.

Is my data syncing?

Open Settings → iCloud Sync on each device. Sync needs you to be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled. Changes can take a minute to appear on your other devices.

Back up or move my data

Use Settings → Backup & Transfer. “Export data…” saves a backup file and “Import data…” restores it (Merge with your data, or Replace all). You can also export a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF report there.
