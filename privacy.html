Privacy Policy

Cash Flow Analyzer · Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version: we don't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud.

What the app stores

Cash Flow Analyzer stores the information you enter: income, commitments and bills, credit card and loan balances, budgets, logged spending, and settings. This data is kept on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs this data between your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. It is encrypted and managed by Apple under your Apple Account. The developer cannot see or access it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive, or have access to your financial data or any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies, and we do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not require an account, and the app does not contact any server operated by the developer.

Features that use your device

  • Face ID / Touch ID optionally locks the app. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and macOS and is never available to the app.
  • Calendar access, if you enable it, adds your payment due dates to your calendar. It is used only for that.
  • Notifications, if enabled, are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Receipt and statement scanning and the Ask feature are processed on your device using Apple's on-device technologies. The images and text you scan or ask about are not sent to us or to any third party.
  • Siri and Shortcuts actions run on your device.
  • Exports and backups (CSV, PDF, JSON) are created only when you ask, and saved or shared where you choose.

Deleting your data

You can delete items in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. To remove synced data, delete it in the app before uninstalling, or manage iCloud storage for the app in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
